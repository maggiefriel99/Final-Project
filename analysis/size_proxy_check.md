# Can complaint stretch stand in for project size?

140 of 201 street, sewer, water, and drainage projects with a mapped footprint sit on a street with nearby complaints in the same category.

- Rank correlation, complaint stretch vs. real project length: **-0.066**
- Rank correlation, complaint stretch vs. budget: -0.124 (real footprint area vs. budget: 0.408)
- Median complaint stretch ÷ project length: 0.76

| Size measure | Out-of-sample R² | 50% range | 80% range | 50% coverage | 80% coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| no size | -0.048 | 3.44x | 12.6x | 47% | 76% |
| real footprint area | 0.108 | 2.94x | 9.3x | 47% | 76% |
| complaint stretch (the app's proxy) | -0.021 | 3.26x | 12.2x | 48% | 76% |

**If the City published project size:** on all 201 infrastructure projects with a footprint, real footprint area raises out-of-sample R² from -0.016 to 0.263 and narrows the 80% range from 15.8x to 10.3x (50% range 3.77x to 3.4x).

**Verdict:** too weak: keep typical-size estimates in the app and report the finding.
